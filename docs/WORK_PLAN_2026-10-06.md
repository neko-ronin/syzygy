# Work plan — 2026-10-06

The owner authorised the six-item street/hero batch, then city richness and
large-world optimisation. The performance target is **this Mac, Chrome,
1600 × 900**, confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-06. The city remains procedural,
WebGPU-only, without runtime npm dependencies or texture assets.

## First: finish the street batch

1. Photograph the actual derived Market Spine Counter Walk in daylight and at
   night. Hold camera, scene, actors, weather, lens and local detail availability;
   change phase and its authored lighting only. Preserve the baseline before
   implementation changes.
2. Restore coherent near pavement/carriageway materials with authored surface
   identity reaching the shader. Judge joints, grain and material scale at 2–3 m.
3. Resolve the raised footway–kerb top–face–lower road relationship.
4. Repair the crowd neighbour query before tuning local separation. Test against
   brute-force spatial truth, then judge independent grounded people and motion.
   D26's later scene/script layer remains a later stage.
5. Use the matched night frame to improve received light where the frontage,
   bodies and passing strip need it.
6. Run required checks; obtain a separate vision verdict on references,
   before/after comparison grids and sidecars; update current status.

Each visual iteration changes one thing. GPU work is serialised through
`tools/orchestrate.mjs`, with a stated two-capture budget per round. Findings,
artifact paths and the round reflection belong in the implementer's report.

## Then: richness and sustained browser performance

Research first-party Insomniac Spider-Man material and Epic Fortnite/Unreal
material in `LARGE_WORLD_RESEARCH.md`. Distinguish the games' documented methods
from general engine capabilities and from adaptations proposed for this city.

Profile before selecting an architectural change. Candidate work includes
predictive asynchronous chunk production, bounded uploads, hierarchical distant
representations, conservative visibility, and instancing repeated small items.
Preserve district silhouettes, deterministic placement, near material richness,
and useful distant geometry. Do not substitute a shorter empty horizon for
meeting the target.

Measure the following at the owner's target size:

- Held street/market and wide-skyline views after an explicitly recorded warm-up.
- Continuous flight through chunk boundaries, fast travel and direction reversal.
- Cold boot separately from steady traversal; no hidden synchronous warm-up in a
  result presented as seamless streaming.
- Quality tier, actual internal render size, draw distance, resident CPU/GPU
  memory estimates, draw/triangle counts, streaming backlog, CPU time, raw frame
  intervals, submitted/completed work and GPU timestamp timings where available.
- Frame-time percentiles and slow-frame counts alongside sustained frame rate.
  `requestAnimationFrame` callbacks and CPU encode speed alone do not prove 60
  rendered frames per second. Queue completion is not direct display telemetry.

The target is a 16.67 ms frame budget and sustained 60 FPS during traversal.
Any benchmark report must say which parts met it and which did not, at the
recorded quality and distance. An adaptive run is reported separately from a
fixed-quality comparison. Visual richness is judged against frames, without
inventing numerical aesthetic scores.
